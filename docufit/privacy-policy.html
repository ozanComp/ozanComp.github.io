DocuFit PDF

Privacy Policy · Effective November 9, 2026

Developer: Ozan Soft
Contact: ozan.soft99@gmail.com

DocuFit PDF helps users convert selected images into PDF documents. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the application.

Document processing

Images selected by the user and PDF documents created from them are processed locally on the user's device. The developer does not operate a server that receives or stores the contents of selected images or generated PDF documents.

Generated PDF files are saved on the user's device. Users can view, share or delete those files using compatible applications and the device's file-management features.

Advertising

The free version uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display banner, rewarded and interstitial advertisements. Google and its partners may process information such as device or advertising identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, app interactions, diagnostic information and advertising data to provide, measure, secure and personalize advertising where permitted.

DocuFit uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and manage advertising consent where required. Available choices may depend on the user's region and device settings. See Google's privacy policy.

Purchases

DocuFit Pro is offered as a one-time in-app purchase through Google Play Billing. Payment information and purchase processing are handled by Google Play. The developer does not receive full payment card details. The application receives purchase status information needed to unlock and restore Pro features.

Permissions and device access

DocuFit uses Android's system media picker so users can choose specific images. It does not request unrestricted access to the user's photo library. Network access is used for advertising, consent and Google Play purchase services.

Children's privacy

DocuFit is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Data retention and deletion

The developer does not maintain user accounts or store document contents on developer-operated servers. Users control locally generated files and may delete them from their device at any time. Data processed independently by Google is subject to Google's retention and deletion controls.

Security

Document contents remain on the device unless the user chooses to share them through another application or service. Network communications used by integrated Google services are protected in transit using standard transport encryption.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when application features, legal requirements or third-party services change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.
