ROADLEDGER

Privacy Policy

Effective: November 10, 2026

Overview

RoadLedger helps you keep vehicle, fuel, service, mileage and expense records on your device. We do not operate a user-account server and do not sell personal information.

Data stored on your device

Vehicle records and preferences are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to a RoadLedger server.

Advertising

The free version uses Google AdMob. Google may process device identifiers, approximate location, diagnostics and interaction data to provide, measure and protect advertising, subject to your consent choices and Google's policies. Where required, RoadLedger displays Google's consent form before requesting ads.

Purchases

RoadLedger Pro is sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes payment information. RoadLedger receives purchase-entitlement status but not your full payment-card details.

Children

RoadLedger is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13.

Choices

You can manage advertising consent where available, reset your advertising ID in Android settings, or remove locally stored information by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.
